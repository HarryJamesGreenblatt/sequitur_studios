# Appendix C — Further Reading (abridged)

> Adapted from Christopher J. Bowen, *Grammar of the Shot* (4th ed.), Appendix C.
> **Scope:** external grounding sources, **reorganized by the department they'd
> serve** in the studio's [architecture](../../../context/architecture.md) — a
> candidate-source shortlist for growing the
> [grounding library](../../INDEX.md) beyond Grammar of the Shot.

The book's flat resource list is regrouped here by production discipline so it
doubles as a roadmap for which sources to import next. (Bibliographic details are
factual pointers; the full original list lives in gitignored `source/`.)

| Department / layer | Candidate source (author) |
|--------------------|---------------------------|
| **Editing / post** *(the clear next layer)* | *Grammar of the Edit* — Bowen; *The Technique of Film and Video Editing* — Dancyger; *FilmCraft: Editing* — Chang |
| Cinematography | *Cinematography: Theory and Practice* — Brown; *DSLR Cinema* — Lancaster; *The Camera Assistant's Manual* — Elkins |
| Lighting | *Motion Picture and Video Lighting* — Brown; *Light — Science and Magic* — Hunter et al. |
| Visual structure / composition | *The Visual Story* — Block |
| Color | *If It's Purple, Someone's Gonna Die* — Bellantoni |
| Production / art design | *The Art Direction Handbook* — Rizzo |
| Directing | *Directing: Film Techniques and Aesthetics* — Rabiger & Hurbis-Cherrier; *Changing Direction* (actors) — DeKoven; *Directing the Story* (storyboarding) — Glebas |
| Screenwriting / story | *The Screenwriter's Roadmap* — Landau |
| Whole-pipeline | *Voice & Vision* — Hurbis-Cherrier |

**Useful tools** referenced: depth-of-field calculators (dofmaster,
omnicalculator), and the ASC (theasc.com) for cinematography practice.

## Studio application

- This is effectively the **import backlog** for the grounding library. *Grammar
  of the Edit* is first (unlocks the editorial/sequence layer); color, directing,
  and story sources map to the still-unfilled departments in
  [`architecture.md`](../../../context/architecture.md).
