## Scope note

Directing is a **spine, not a single-department source.** Its center of gravity is
the **Director** — deepening a role that today only *borrows* Grammar of the Shot
Ch. 1 and, in code, merely reconciles the crew's contributions (0014). But it also
seeds cells no other source touches: the **Production Designer** (Ch. 23), the
**Casting/actors** dimension (Ch. 18–20, unmodeled today), and **delivery**
(Ch. 37). Where chapters overlap existing sources — post (Ch. 31–36 vs *Grammar of
the Edit*), sound/music (Ch. 35 vs Rose) — treat Directing as the **director's-eye
complement**, not a replacement. Story chapters (3–8) overlap **The Screenwriter's
Taxonomy**: Directing gives dramaturgical *craft*, the Taxonomy gives a *classification
system*.
