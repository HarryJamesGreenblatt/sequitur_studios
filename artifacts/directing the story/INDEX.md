# Directing the Story — grounding index

Francis Glebas, *Directing the Story: Professional Storytelling and Storyboarding
Techniques for Live Action and Animation* (Routledge/Focal Press,
ISBN 9780240810768). This is a **cross-cutting story-craft reference** for the
studio's **Director** and **Screenwriter** seats — a story artist's manual on the
**story → image bridge**: how a dramatic beat becomes a *readable* image. It is
**not** a new department: like Rabiger's *Directing*, it attaches to seats that
already exist (see [`0041`](../../context/storyline/0041-staging-directing-the-story-and-production-design.md)),
strengthening the Director `PersonaJudgment` (the **B** tier) and the Screenwriter
*treatment* — it adds no `crew/` module.

## Scope note

This source fills a **niche none of the story sources fills**: Rabiger's *Directing*
gives broad directing craft, Williams' *Taxonomy* gives a *classification system*,
and Paez & Jew's *Professional Storyboarding* gives board *mechanics* — Glebas gives
the **visual dramatic storytelling** in between (clarity of staging, directing the
eye, images that speak). Watch the **story-space crowding** flagged in `0041`: this
is *depth, not redundancy* **only** if the deferred abridgement stays surgical —
targeting Glebas' distinctive visual-storytelling material and skipping what merely
restates Rabiger. Overlaps to reconcile at abridgement: the **story spine** (Glebas
Ch. 5, 12 ↔ Taxonomy Ch. 6 ↔ Directing Ch. 5); **staging a beat / clarity** (Glebas
Ch. 7–8 ↔ Directing Ch. 10–11 ↔ Prof. Storyboarding Ch. 5–6).

> **Naming guard.** This folder (`directing the story/`, Glebas) is **not**
> `directing/` (Rabiger & Hurbis-Cherrier) — one is a story-artist's
> visual-storytelling manual, the other the Director-centric film-directing spine.
